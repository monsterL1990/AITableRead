# AI readers and prompts

At the top you can configure the AI readers you have now, and the prompts used to generate text.

![Figure 6: Reader settings](/docs-assets/fig6-readers.jpeg)

Some readers are already set up. You can edit them, hide them (they then do not show in the reader column while writing), or delete them.

At the bottom you can describe someone in one sentence, and optionally upload attachments, to generate a reader in one step. You can also write the prompt yourself and set the reader up that way.

The writing prompts are where you adjust the prompt text for each step.
