# Getting started with WeiDu

## Start a manuscript

After you have signed in and finished the AI settings, click "New manuscript" to start a writing project and the workflow.

![Figure 1: New manuscript](/docs-assets/fig1-new.png)

A first setup screen appears. Everything here can be changed later:

![Figure 2: New manuscript form](/docs-assets/fig2-new-form.png)

The screen has these fields:

1. **Title**: the title of this writing project. An export from the project is named with it.
2. **Where to start**: WeiDu can start from three different points in the writing process, and the prompt used is different for each. Once you start, this cannot be changed inside the project.
3. **Idea**: the initial prompt, idea, outline, or manuscript you want the content generated from.
4. **Minimum length / maximum length**: the target length of the finished prose (the outline is not held to this length). You can change it any time inside the project.

When you are done, click Start to begin the project.

## Starting from an idea or an outline

If you start from an idea or an outline, you land on the "Idea → Outline" screen.

![Figure 3: Start from an idea or an outline](/docs-assets/fig3-idea-outline.jpeg)

If you started from an idea, the idea shows on the left. If you started from an outline, the outline shows on the right.

If you started from an idea, click Generate outline under the left side. The outline is generated from the idea in the window on the right.

If you started from an outline, click Confirm this version as final at the bottom, and you go to the "Outline → manuscript" screen.

On that screen you can have the AI readers read the outline you generated or entered, and then give their own comments. Click Ask every reader who isn't hidden, on the right of the readers. Those readers start reading. A progress bar appears at the top. When every reader has finished, each comment shows under the reader area, like this:

![Figure 4: Reader notes](/docs-assets/fig4-reader-feedback.png)

You can expand a reader's comment to read it. Then you choose whether to adopt it. Only after you adopt the comments you want can you click Revise once from the adopted reader notes, at the lower left. You can also click Discuss on a comment and say what you want changed.

When you decide to adopt comments and revise the outline from them, click Revise once from the adopted reader notes. The outline is revised from the comments you adopted. A progress bar appears at the top until the outline is done, and the outline window refreshes. You can send the new outline to the readers again and revise again.

Besides the readers, you can send your own comment. That is the "your revision notes" area at the bottom. Type it and click Revise once from this note. The outline is revised from your comment too.

When you are sure the outline is fine, click Confirm this version as final at the bottom. You go to the "Outline → manuscript" screen.

## Outline → manuscript, or starting from a full manuscript

![Figure 5: Start from polish or a full manuscript](/docs-assets/fig5-polish.jpeg)

The "Outline → manuscript" page and the "full manuscript → polish" screen are basically the same. The outline or manuscript goes in on the left. On the right the AI writes the result from that input and from your comments or the AI readers' comments.

One thing to watch: the first result often misses the length, and often misses the outline. That is why this screen has a Quality check area at the bottom. If the length is wrong, or it doesn't follow the outline, click Run a quality check there first. After it has read the piece, the check gives you a comment.

Click revise again from the checklist, and the new manuscript can usually meet the length and the outline.

But when the length target is high (more than 3000), a new result can hit the count and still be padded. That is when having the AI readers review the manuscript, and then revising, matters most. After the quality check, have the AI readers read the manuscript once and revise once. That gets the best result.

Revising from AI readers, and revising yourself, work the same way as above. I won't go through it again.

When the final version is confirmed, click Export prose at the top right. The prose is downloaded as an md file. Clicking confirm again after that enters polish.

The project stays on your desk. You can open it any time, and you can still change the steps in the middle.
